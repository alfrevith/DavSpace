A curious mind. A practical approach.

I'm David, a Salvadoran student with an analytical mind and a strong creative side. Mathematics, technology, and writing give me different ways to understand a problem or express an idea.

I'm drawn to things I can explore deeply, from the way a system fits together to the way a story takes shape. I enjoy connecting what I know with something I haven't tried yet.
