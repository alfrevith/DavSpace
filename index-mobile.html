Pet's Health

Where It All Began

An idea for better pet care became my first exploration of interfaces, teamwork, and application logic.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Concept Development, UI Design, Basic Application Logic

Behind this chapter +

The challenge

Turning an ambitious pet-care idea into an early interactive prototype.

The work & what it taught me

Pet's Health was one of my first experiences in software development. The project aimed to create a digital platform that made pet care easier by organizing medical records, veterinary appointments, pet communities, and pet-related services and products.

CHAPTER 03 2025

POT-C

Beyond the Screen

With Klani, our composting prototype, I stepped beyond interfaces and into electronics and physical making.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Front-End Development, 3D Printing, Hardware Support

Behind this chapter +

The challenge

Bringing an environmental idea into both a digital experience and a physical prototype.

The work & what it taught me

POT-C was an environmental technology project developed with my team at ¡Supérate! Fundación Poma.

The project introduced Klani, a smart compost bin prototype designed to transform organic household waste, such as fruit peels, vegetable scraps, and eggshells, into natural compost through a five-phase process.

My primary responsibility was developing the front end of its informational website using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS.

The website included interactive environmental statistics, charts, image galleries, and a 3D visualization of Klani.

I also helped with the 3D printing of physical components and the assembly of the prototype's internal electronic circuitry.

The project was presented during Expo 2025, Roots of Legacy.

WHAT STAYED WITH ME

“I realized that technology doesn't have to stay inside a screen. Programming, electronics, and creativity can work together to bring physical ideas to life.”

CHAPTER 04 2026

Plant Bud

Connecting Everything

A collaborative platform where AI, plant care, and local services became parts of one experience.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Front-End Development, Back-End Development, AI Integration, Teamwork

Behind this chapter +

The challenge

Connecting many features and technologies into one coherent application.

The work & what it taught me

Plant Bud became one of my most ambitious collaborative projects, developed alongside a team from ¡Supérate! Fundación Poma.

The platform was designed to make plant care easier through artificial intelligence and digital services.

Its main features included AI-powered plant health analysis through photographs, personalized care recommendations, plant information, AI-assisted garden redesign using uploaded images, professional gardener hiring, nearby nursery discovery, and a reward points system for payments made through the platform.

I contributed to both front-end and back-end development, helping integrate different technologies and functionalities into a larger application.

We also presented the project at Expo Tech.

WHAT STAYED WITH ME

“I learned that building a complex application isn't just about developing individual features. It's about connecting everything into a meaningful and functional experience.”

CHAPTER 05 2026

Beyond

Creating with Purpose

My own experience preparing for AVANZO became a free learning tool for other students.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Independent Development, Front-End and Back-End Logic, Educational Technology

Behind this chapter +

The challenge

Turning the challenge of studying several subjects into useful, independent practice.

The work & what it taught me

Beyond was born during my final year of high school while preparing for the AVANZO 2026 exam in El Salvador.

After experiencing how challenging it could be to study multiple subjects at once, I decided to create a free educational platform that could help other students prepare more effectively.

The platform includes interactive practice across Mathematics, Language and Literature, Natural Sciences, and Social Studies.

Students can complete 20-question timed exams with a 60-minute limit, take extensive 100-question diagnostic assessments, receive feedback, and learn from detailed explanations of incorrect answers.

Beyond allowed me to apply my programming experience to a project I developed independently, combining educational content, interactive assessment systems, and user-centered design.

WHAT STAYED WITH ME

“I learned that something I experienced personally could become the starting point for a tool capable of helping others.”

My Portfolio

Building My Own Space

A personal space where my technical and creative sides finally meet.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Independent Web Development, UI/UX, Interactive Experiences
